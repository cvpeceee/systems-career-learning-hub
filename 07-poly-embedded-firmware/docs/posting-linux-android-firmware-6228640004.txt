About The Role
We’re looking for a 
Linux/Android
&nbsp;
Embedded Firmware Developer
 who’s passionate about software design and development, software architecture, and Linux technology, to help us build the next generation of video conferencing devices. If you thrive in a collaborative, agile environment and love solving complex problems, we’d love to meet you.
What you’ll do
Design and develop innovative firmware for Android- and/or Linux-based video conferencing devices.
Build and enhance platform capabilities below the framework layer.
Lead the development of secure, scalable, and high-performance embedded C/C++ applications.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define architecture, standards, and best practices.
Troubleshoot and resolve complex system-level issues in embedded environments.
Contribute to project planning, documentation, and testing strategies.
Mentor junior engineers and share your expertise across the team.
Drive continuous improvement through agile and lean development practices.
What you bring
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience.
5+ years of experience in embedded software development
Proficiency in embedded C/C++.
Experience with firmware development and debugging in embedded environments.
Knowledge in below areas is a big plus:
Networking/Wifi
Security
USB
Video encoding/decoding (H.264, H.265, SVC)
HDMI
Bluetooth
Strong communication skills and a collaborative mindset.
A passion for learning, innovation, and delivering high-quality solutions.
Understanding of Android internals and Linux is preferred.
